# Messaging provider logos

These official assets identify the corresponding messaging providers. Trademark rights remain
with their respective owners; use is subject to each provider's brand guidelines. The artwork,
colors, backgrounds and proportions are unchanged.

The bundled SVGs add only the nonvisual accessible titles `<title>Mattermost</title>` and
`<title>Discord</title>`. Removing each exact title recovers its downloaded upstream bytes.
The PNG is unchanged. Source hashes below identify the official downloads; bundled hashes
include the SVG titles. These assets were retrieved on 2026-10-15.

The files are in `frontend/public/logos/` in the source, and the build copies them into the
app's bundled `logos/` folder (`src/viki_agent/resources/ui/logos/`).

- `mattermost.svg`: the instrument logomark in `#222222`, with a transparent background.
  [Brand guidelines](https://handbook.mattermost.com/operations/operations/company-processes/publishing/publishing-guidelines/brand-and-visual-design-guidelines).
  [Original SVG](https://4287631337-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-LorODNCmgMboJfZ7MPT-3109903374%2Fuploads%2Fgit-blob-6c7ab89bca0b50a48f1d35edf13e76fd6faf5b43%2Ficon.svg?alt=media).
- `discord.svg`: the Clyde symbol in official blurple `#5865F2`, with a transparent background.
  [Brand guidelines](https://discord.com/branding).
  [Original SVG](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6257adef93867e50d84d30e2/66e3d80db9971f10a9757c99_Symbol.svg).
- `slack.png`: the full-color octothorpe on its original opaque aubergine background, 256×256.
  [Media kit](https://slack.com/media-kit) and [brand terms](https://slack.com/terms-of-service/slack-brand).
  [Original PNG](https://a.slack-edge.com/80588/marketing/img/meta/slack_hash_256.png), referenced
  by the media-kit page as its Open Graph image.
