# The window at a glance

A walk through the seven parts of the VIKI Agent window, in the order you will use them.

## The left rail
Your starting point. **New session** opens a chat. Below it sit the areas of the app, such as **Chat**, **Vault**, **Agents** and **Routines**; each appears when that part of the app is available. **Search sessions** finds past chats, your chats are listed under it, and the workspace name at the bottom opens the workspace settings. In a narrow window the rail becomes a drawer: open it with the button at the top left.

## Start a chat
Select **New session**, or press `Alt`+`N`. If Claude Code is not ready, the button does nothing and a note above the chat says why.

## The message box
Type and press `Enter` to send; `Shift`+`Enter` adds a line. Type `/` for commands and skills, or press `↑` in an empty box to recall your last message. Paste or drop an image to attach it. While VIKI replies, **Stop** or `Esc` interrupts it. Drag the line just above the box to make it taller; double-click that line, or press Enter on it, to reset its height.

## The conversation
VIKI's replies appear here, with tool steps folded into groups you can expand. A request that needs you shows a card with **Allow once** and **Deny**. Under a finished reply, chips show what VIKI read. Open chats are tabs in the conversation header; in a wide window, drag a tab to a pane's edge to see up to four chats side by side.

## The right panel
The right panel shows what needs you, your Bots, what subagents are doing, the files used in this chat, your skills, your note counts and how full the chat's context window is. In a narrow window it becomes a drawer: open it with the panel button near the top right.

## The top bar
At the top right, `?` opens this help, and the gear (**App menu**) holds **Settings**, **Switch workspace**, **Diagnostics** and your data tools.

## The status bar
Along the bottom, the Git chip shows the branch and state of your vault or code folder; click it for details. A plan-limit meter and short notices follow when there is something to report, such as Claude Code being signed out or Git missing. While the right panel is hidden, your note counts and how full the context window is show here instead. App readiness and Claude Code's version are in **Diagnostics**.
